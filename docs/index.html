WhatsApp Google Classroom Assistant

A personal automation tool that keeps WhatsApp study groups up to date with Google Classroom — no one has to open Classroom to know what's happening.

This website is for information only. There is nothing to sign into here, and no accounts, cookies or analytics.

What the application does

  • Live auto-sync — new Classroom announcements, coursework and materials are summarised and posted into WhatsApp groups selected by the operator.
  • Deadline reminders — upcoming assignments are checked automatically and announced before they are due.
  • On-demand commands — group members can ask for their courses, recent posts or soon-due work with simple chat commands (for example !due or !recent).
  • Documents to chat — files shared in Classroom can be requested and delivered straight into WhatsApp, including as a private (direct) message.
  • AI study assistant — a chat assistant that can answer questions and summarise the group's recent conversation.

Data handling

The application reads data from Google Classroom and Google Drive using read-only access only, and posts summaries and files into the WhatsApp groups configured by the operator. Full details: Privacy Policy.
